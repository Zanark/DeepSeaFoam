The defining decision

Depth is not the same as darkness.

DeepSeaFoam does not wash every surface in teal or substitute generic charcoal. It creates hierarchy with two exact large-area colors.

Hierarchy in practice

Signals stay small so they can stay meaningful.

The accent is not a wallpaper color. Warm ivory is not a default white. Red is not permanent decoration. Each appears only where its role earns attention.

  • Interactionfocus, links, active outlines
  • Emphasisheadings, notices, selected text
  • Cautionlocalized warning indicators
  • Exceptionerror boundaries and diagnostics

New roles are labeled as new roles.
