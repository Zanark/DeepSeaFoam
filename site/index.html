Character, not decoration

Calm. Considered. Technically capable.

Familiar waters. Familiar light.

If the sea feels like home, these colors have a familiar rhythm: deep water, small signals, and the warmth of a lamp after dark. It is a nautical association, not a claim that every sailor sees or feels the same way.

Solarized lovers will recognize the blue-green shadows, warm ivory, and purposeful accents: our mist text and warm emphasis are its base1 and base2. DeepSeaFoam keeps that family resemblance, then gives the workspace more depth and the signals the vividness of a well-loved terminal.

“Solarized reduces brightness contrast but, unlike many low contrast colorschemes, retains contrasting hues”

Ethan Schoonover, Solarized — design rationale, 2011.

“allowing time for dark adaptation when undertaking lookouts at night.”

Maritime and Coastguard Agency, night-lookout guidance — a reminder that lighting matters, not evidence for any particular palette.

Comfort remains personal. A 2013 visual-acuity and proofreading study reported “A positive polarity advantage was found for both age groups.” That means dark text on light backgrounds. None of these sources tests DeepSeaFoam; WCAG contrast guidance informs our text/background pairings, not a universal eye-health promise.
